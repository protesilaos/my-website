For this entry in the series, I have picked an emotional song titled
_Coral tear_ from the solo collection of songmaker Thanos
Anestopoulos: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kOTQ4Lf0e38>.

Thanos was a member of the band _Diafan Krina_. Roughly two years ago,
I wrote an interpretation of one of their songs:
<https://protesilaos.com/interpretations/2024-06-29-krina-idle-days/>.

_Coral tear_ preserves some of the melancholy that characterises the
work of _Diafana Krina_, which makes me think that Thanos was the soul
of the band.

At any rate, this is a wonderful piece with inspiring lyrics. Below is
the Greek text, my faithful translation of it, and further comments.

```
Δάκρυ κοραλλένιο

Ερμηνεία:  Θάνος Ανεστόπουλος
Στίχοι:    Θάνος Ανεστόπουλος
Μουσική:   Θάνος Ανεστόπουλος


Κοίτα να ντύνεσαι καλά
ήρθ'ο χειμώνας, πιες ακόμα μια γουλιά
Σου'χω φυλάξει μια αγκαλιά
και μια χάρτινη βαρκούλα μια σταλιά

Κοίτα στους τοίχους το λίγο φως
Είμαι η σκιά σου που σου χαμογελά
Τρέξε στην πόλη και βάλε φωτιά
Θα είμαι δίπλα σου όπου κι αν πας

Κοίτα να χτίσεις μια φωλιά
κι εκεί να κρύψεις λίγο ήλιο απ'τα παλιά
Σου'χω φυλάξει μια θάλασσα
και ένα δάκρυ κοραλλένιο μια σταλιά

Κοίτα τα χέρια σου, αντέχουν πολλά
Διώξ'τα σκοτάδια σου και κοίτα ψηλά
Μείνε στην έρημο μες στη φωτιά
Θα είμαι δίπλα σου όπου κι αν πας
```

```
Coral tear

Singer:  Thanos Anestopoulos
Lyrics:  Thanos Anestopoulos
Music:   Thanos Anestopoulos


Look to dress well
winter has come, take another sip
I have stored for you a hug
and a tiny paper boat

Look at the faint light on the walls
I am the shadow smiling at you
Run to the town and set a fire
I shall be beside you wherever you go

Look to build a nest
and there to hide a bit of sun from the past
I have stored for you a sea
and a tiny coral tear

Look at your hand, they have endured a lot
Cast away your darkness and look up high
Stay in the desert within the fire
I shall be beside you wherever you go
```

I imagine the poetic first person as a guardian figure. It is the one
who cares deeply about the wellness of the poetic "you". The guardian
appears to be reminding the unnamed second person of its own moments
of respite, of its achievements, and its innate capacity to be hopeful.

In the song, hope is represented as feelings that can be stored, the
past which persists as a matter of choice, and even the teardrop which
may solidify so that it is not lost.

The titular coral tear is the symbol of this hope. In that which is
ephemeral, the tear, it identifies the potential for permanence.
Perhaps this cannot be done: teardrops do not transform into corals.
Maybe impermanence is all we experience, though one can still reason
about transcendence and thus the poet employs the metaphor of the
teardrop to remind us of what we can at all times fathom.

Hopeful is the person who does not rush to early conclusions. Do not
say "this is impossible" when you cannot have a definitive answer. Let
the tears flow in earnest, as you accept impermanence. The honesty of
your experience may leave such an impression on you that permanence no
longer seems alien: it stays with you and it defines you thenceforth.

I suspect many a poet employs "you" as a proxy for their self. We can
interpret _Coral tear_ along those lines as well. Sometimes we need to
remind ourselves to take it easy, to not fixate on what is troubling
us, to recognise that hope remains with us and that we have the
potential to think of our case anew.

As I have implied in cryptic fashion in my publication [Welcome to my
world, which is your world](https://protesilaos.com/commentary/2026-09-06-welcome-my-world-your-world/)
(2026-09-06), the creator cannot share the totality of their world
with anyone. If my creativity is a fountain whose waters flow
downstream, you may trace everything back to the source, at which
point you find that the spring comes from the depths of the mountain.
You are so close to the source, yet forever away from it.

In the innermost world of the creator, at the depths of this
loneliness, hope manifests as a belief in the possibility that someone
attracted to it, someone who has had another taste of those waters,
can align their mind perfectly with the creator's. Only then the two
are one.

Should that happen, however, the creator has not found another: the
you is the I and the distinction collapses into itself.
