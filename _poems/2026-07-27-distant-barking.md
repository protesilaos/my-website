---
title: "Poem: Distant is the barking"
excerpt: "Just read the poem. No further comment."
---

```
Distant is the barking
that ignites hope
of a reunion
As weeks fly by
and although I know
you are not approaching
I still pull the curtain
hoping to witness
your innocent eyes
once again
or at least
learn from the sparrows
that you roam the wilds
I am not prepared
to traverse
```
