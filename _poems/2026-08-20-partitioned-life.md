---
title: "Poem: Partitioned life"
excerpt: "Just read the poem. No further comment."
---

```
Partitioned life you seek
of joy minus the suffering
Homesick you thus are
for a land that never is
aspiring to a state
you shall not be present in
This rugged  boat of yours
can sail to the furthest edges
where glaciers never melt
and fire burns eternal
only to demonstrate
that foam and depth
here and there
are one
```
