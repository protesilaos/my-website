I designed the basis of this theme during a live stream earlier today:
<https://protesilaos.com/codelog/2026-10-31-emacs-live-ef-themes-halloween/>.
Then I followed it up with refinements to colour values and palette
mappings. The light counterpart of this theme will be the subject of
further development work, maybe tomorrow or next week.

When I include "light" or "dark" in the theme's name, it means that I
have envisaged it as part of a pair. Most themes are not designed as
pairs though, as certain colours cannot work in both cases.

## Coming in version 2.3.0 (next stable release)

I may still tweak it further, but the character is already set. It
captures the style I had in mind and I am happy with the result.

Remember that since version `2.0.0`, the `ef-themes` are built on top
of my `modus-themes`. This means that they are highly customisable,
support a wide range of packages and face groups, and are extensively
tested down to the finest details.

Enjoy!

* * *

The `ef-themes` are a collection of light and dark themes for GNU
Emacs that provide colourful ("pretty") yet legible options for users
who want something with a bit more flair than the `modus-themes` (also
designed by me).

+ Package name (GNU ELPA): `ef-themes`
+ Official manual: <https://protesilaos.com/emacs/ef-themes>
+ Change log: <https://protesilaos.com/emacs/ef-themes-changelog>
