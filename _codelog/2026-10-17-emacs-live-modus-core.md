Today, Saturday, 17 October 2026, at 14:30 Europe/Athens, I will work
on my modus-themes for Emacs.

About a year ago I turned them into a framework that theme developers
can rely on. Lots of themes have been built on top of Modus since
then.

What I want to do this year is extract the core Modus that other
themes need into its own library. This way, we can have a separation
of concerns between what is the framework proper and what are
modus-themes (modus-operandi, modus-vivendi, etc.).

I expect to work on this for 3 hours or more.

I will have the chat open in case someone has a question about what I
am programming or ANYTHING else they might want to ask me.
