In this 1-hour video I read from the opening chapter of Nicomachean
Ethics, by Aristotle, and comment at length on themes of ranking goals
in projects, of having a notion of success versus failure, of
determining what we are actually working towards, of what exactly it
means for some realities to be instituted as such, and of the one sin
from which all others derive which is the sin of exaggeration.

Next time I will comment on eudaemonia, which is mistranslated as
"happiness", but we will get there.
