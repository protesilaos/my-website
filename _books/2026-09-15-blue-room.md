---
title: "The blue room"
excerpt: "Short story about a blue room, which is so much more than a room."
---

The blue room draws you in. It offers the refuge you seek. Its
material construction diffuses all noise. You are neither disturbed
nor distracted by what transpires in your midst. Such is the comfort
it provides.

Days do not matter within its confines. What was, shall be. Time loses
its meaning when all you can do is stare at those walls. Its passage
is not paused though. Each day leaves its mark on the body. Every
second is impressed on the skin to show you, whenever you muster the
courage to look at the mirror, just how late you have been.

The blue room remembers your moments. Records it keeps to remind those
passing by how long it has remained standing. In its space are stored
memories of bygone days. It keeps track of your perfect score in a
particular category: of dreams never to materialise. Those are your
wants: the adventures you did not embark on. They are the characters
you never became and the roles you did not assume. It stood there
watching them go unnoticed by the travellers of this world.

You appear not, when all is a single colour, no matter how actively
you try and how strongly you want it. There is no pattern to be
discerned. When you speak, they wonder how is it that a building can
have a voice. People are afraid of haunted places, after all. Instead
of inferring that you might be there somewhere, they act in response
to their deep-seated fears. You are irrelevant in your invisibility.

The blue room absorbs it all. It has inexhaustible patience. It wants
you to remain in place. Only then can it perform its function of
protecting you, of shielding you from whatever danger lurks in the
corner. From your daily routine it will remove any sense of urgency.
You shall not go anywhere. There is no need to. What you have is
complete. It shall suffice for a lifetime.

Plans for tomorrow can be revised to become aspirations for the week
after, which in turn will be rolled over to a future date that remains
elusive. The hopes are distorted accordingly, with pragmatic
considerations woven into their narrative. A few are enough to bring
an abrupt end to the story that would otherwise develop. Not even
there do you become another.

The blue room loves you. It tells you that it has desire for you
alone. Special you feel when you are noticed, even if it is only by a
structure. It has a life of its own. A logic that defies and then
overrides your volition. It enters your mind as shapeshifter
extraordinaire to become friend and lover whose name you keep
forgetting.

The blue room questions your judgement. There is nowhere to go when
all you can think of is informed by monotonous surroundings. The door
is right there. Step outside whenever you want only to discover that
you jump back in. Without a plan, you revert to your default
condition, effectively working against the goal you never defined. As
you do that, hope is lost. It feels you are standing while running.

The blue room is you. It extends to form a fine line which connects at
the other end to become a circle. There is no outside, no inside, no
before, no after. It is the everlasting and ubiquitous presence of a
hue; the tyranny of one. You switch off the lights to not notice it
anymore. Yet you know that it and you are the same.
