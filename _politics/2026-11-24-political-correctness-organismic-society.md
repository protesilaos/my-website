Last week I was at a group gathering where I talked mostly about
geopolitics. The topics I discussed are already covered in previous
essays. I will not expound on them herein. Instead, I want to comment
on political correctness followed by my view of groups and society
at-large as an organism.

## Freedom of expression brings correctness of thought

The gathering was at a cafeteria. There was a gym close by. We joined
four or five tables together and sat around them. A gym-goer who was
walking by remarked how "this is hardcore stuff". We were all calm and
easygoing. Though you could tell that something serious was happening.
Everyone was respectful and focused, which indeed is "hardcore" when
compared to quotidian affairs.

My analysis, which went on for about ten minutes, focused on Europe's
geopolitical realities and how Cyprus fits in that calculus. I was
essentially saying that the Western empire has no problem replacing us
with other people: all they are interested in is control of the wider
region. My concluding remark was that "in this world we have big guns
for our enemies and bigger guns for our friends".

In the discussion that followed, one of the organisers asked me how
can we have any hope given that, in his words, "everybody out there is
a piece of meat". These are the kind of politically incorrect
statements you always hear when you discuss politics face-to-face with
people who are not in the business of politics.

"Everybody has positive traits", I said, "they just need to find the
right purpose". To me, everybody counts. It is a matter of giving each
person a role in which they can contribute towards a common goal.
Leadership, then, is about making the most out of what you have.

The fact that there was freedom of expression at the table was
especially helpful. I think dialogue, the actual exchange where you
can get punched in the face, not the pretence of tolerance in front of
the cameras, is the only way to develop correctness of thought.
Someone voices an unfiltered opinion. They are not faking it. Now you
know exactly what they truly value, provided you have been paying
attention.

To him, I think, "the meat" is a reflection of his own pessimism. He
sees useless people everywhere because he is too deep in his own
self-enfeeblement. By showing him courage and conviction, both with my
presence and my words, I effectively instructed him to get out of that
tunnel. He wants to do this again and we will organise another event
soon, which will be even bigger this time.

## Groups as organisms

Another point I made along those lines is that "I do not believe in
leaders and passive followers" because "I expect everyone to do what
they can and to stand together with their community". Which brings me
to the topics of how I conceptualise groups of people as emergent
organisms. This I did not discuss during the meeting, though it always
informs my commentary.

Groups dynamically constitute themselves into organisms the moment
their parts labour towards a singular objective. For this is what an
organism is: the concentration of vitality in pursuit of a goal, hence
the organisation of the set of parts in light of the objective.

An organism is _homoclite_ (ομόκλιτος), meaning that there is a common
inclination, thus integration and unity. A group that is _heteroclite_
(ετερόκλιτος), of diverse inclinations, is in disunity and is disintegrating.

Essentially, an organism is an instance of communication. For there to
be parts that organise themselves in pursuit of a given objective,
there has to be a transmission mechanism that makes exchange and
attendant feedback loops possible. Put differently, an organism is
constituted through language and consists in language.

## The transmission mechanism

In interpersonal affairs, the transmission mechanism encompasses all
the signals we rely on to disseminate information and derive meaning
through our faculties. Words are not our only language: that is too
narrow a domain. We also have smells and appearances, for example: how
you are physically says a lot about you, your overall lifestyle, your
character and values, and much more.

At the meeting, for instance, I was sitting next to people as one
among equals. I was wearing simple clothes, just as everybody else. I
talked loudly and clearly, to the point where the rest of the
cafeteria turned quiet. What I said is beside the point. Even without
knowing Greek, you would feel the vibe simply by observing the scene.

Organisms preserve their vitality, or anyhow leak less of it, through
efficiency, which involves the transmission mechanism. They economise
on energy such that they minimise intensive operations and re-use as
much of the available products as possible. For groups of peoples,
this is done through imitation. One person comes up with a seemingly
useful pattern of behaviour, like an idea, a method, an invention, and
others adopt it without having to derive it from scratch themselves.

## Energy and efficiency

This is no different than how a computer program works by setting a
variable to the return value of a computation to then reference that
variable instead of computing the same value over and over again.
A wasteful program that keeps computing everything all the time will
eventually run out of computer resources, forcing the system to crash.

It is analogous for people at the personal and interpersonal
dimensions. For a given person, them being wasteful with their energy
will exhaust them temporarily and shall lead to their structural
disempowerment over the long-term. As for groups, if everyone sits
down to, say, prove a mathematical theorem then we will not have
anybody do other kinds of work, such as to gather resources, prepare
food, process metals, drive trucks, sing songs, take care of the young
and the elderly... In other words, the society collapses.

Imitation is not necessarily about creating one-to-one copies. It can
be made manifest as inspiration, adaptation, reformulation and
potential clarification, extension, and continuation. Imitation is how
groups make best use of what they already have. Here is a schematic
representation using pseudo-code where capital letters denote
variables:

```
IDEA   = <<here is the result of some intensive computation>>
DETAIL = <<another computation here>>

if WORK  do  IDEA
if HOME  do (IDEA + DETAIL)
if SPORT do (IDEA - DETAIL)
...
```

Compare that to the inefficient approach, which already looks more
busy:

```
if WORK  do  <<here is the result of some intensive computation>>
if HOME  do (<<here is the result of some intensive computation>> + <<another computation here>>)
if SPORT do (<<here is the result of some intensive computation>> - <<another computation here>>)
...
```

## Resilience through diversity

From this we understand how the diversity of people is not a condition
to be fixed: it being this way is a matter of efficiency at the level
of the organism: societies, humanity as a whole, and greater levels
still.

Imagine a society of philosophers. They talk without ever acting out
of it. They have no dual role: they are not philosophers and
engineers, philosophers and doctors, philosophers and artists. No.
They are just philosophers. Imagine that god forsaken place.

- We need to build a bridge. Any thoughts?
- Make it stable.
- What does "stable" even mean?

Having too much of a certain quality, such as the thoughtfulness of a
philosopher, does not increase its utility accordingly. After a
certain point, it might become detrimental to the whole. This is true
even at the personal level: a person's life will involve at least
somewhat diverse activities, such as work and leisure, else it will
become unsustainable.

"Sustainable" is nature's way of saying "pay attention, as this is the
right approach".

We thus think in terms of degrees and in accordance with the
distribution of skills among members of a group. We then make the
appropriate judgement calls to decide whether we are working towards a
common goal or are moving in different directions.

This insight also helps each person appreciates their role as part of
something greater than themselves. Instead of belittling their
contributions, they understand that even the smallest amount of effort
is important. If a cog in the machine stops working, the entire
machine cannot function at its peak anymore or will not work at all.

## Finding purpose in life

A person with no purpose will be miserable. Without verbalising it,
they suffer from the fact that their vitality is lost to the
environment. Similarly, a nation without an idea of where it is going
is destined to a life of degeneracy.

I know from experience that courage is contagious. I also understand
the significance of doing things the slow and steady way. I want
people to be involved and to have a real sense that they are part of a
group. It is important for all of us to feel that we are converging
and becoming something greater than each of us in isolation.

You can sense the organism taking form only when you get involved. It
will scare away those who are not prepared for the new experience.
Though it shall invigorate the rest. That is the power impulse we can
benefit from.
