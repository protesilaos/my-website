analysed at length in [Gigantism and Europe's far-right transformation](https://protesilaos.com/politics/2026-09-08-europe-far-right-transformation/)
(2026-09-08). The gist of what I wrote is that those liberals who
today shed crocodile tears about the recrudescence of the far-right
will be the enforcers of far-right policies tomorrow.

## Compassion requires understanding

Locals in any given culture who profess tolerance of immigrants
typically do so from a position of ideology, not lived experience.
They are perfectly fine with migration, in abstract, because it does
not harm their vital interests, but they are not exposed to the
struggles an immigrant faces. At some basic level, they cannot relate
to what it is like to be a foreigner in general and a foreigner in
their own society in particular.

The average liberal thinker is comfortably middle class and privileged
in ways whose extent they are not aware of. They were not forced by
economic hardship to remain silent in the face of blatant
exploitation. They did not live outside the private banking system and
did not have to rely on the acquiescence of others. They were not
displaced from their homeland to begin with.

This does not, ipso facto, make the locals malevolent. It merely
introduces a barrier that can only be overcome by becoming aware of it
and then working to move past it.

The menial jobs I have done were always among migrants, with only the
bosses being locals. As a bartender, I worked mostly with Eastern
Europeans. In construction sites, I collaborated primarily with
Syrians. In agriculture, I was together with people from the Indian
subcontinent. Each group has its own niche.

Whenever I was lectured about "tolerance" was from those who have not
used a pickax in their life; those who were not forced to work until
their palms would start bleeding. That is what teaches you tolerance
for good, not books.

## Beware of comfortable ideologues

A labourer among labourers feels at home because each person
understands the other's predicament, despite their otherwise diverse
cultural backgrounds. The problems arise when the person has to
integrate in the broader local society, which now includes the parvenu
and rich classes.

I discovered as much when I worked among leftists and other
progressives of a Western European background at the European
Parliament and beyond in the 2010s. With a couple of exceptions, they
shared the same profile of the economically well-off, highly educated
figure, who pontificates about workers the way parrots talk about
programming.

My colleagues would comment in the most polite fashion on my "North
African looks" and encourage me to wear expensive clothes because with
those I "looked impressive". I am confident about my appearance (I am
confident in general) and will appreciate a fine piece of clothing.
Though this otherwise innocuous language hints at a person's
assumptions and default cultural programming.

What they were actually saying, again without bad intention, is that
my path to mainstream acceptance passed through money. With enough
wealth or pretence thereof I too could become invisible in phase one
and attractive in phase two.

Invisibility is not a matter of skin complexion per se. In the West,
"whitening", if we are to call it thus in the interest of brevity, is
a process of assimilation into the dominant cultural group. If you do
what they do and say what they say, if you too worship money, then
they will recognise you as their equal. Start thinking for yourself,
however, dismiss their idols, and deviate one bit from their worldview
only to learn that now you have an appearance that stands out, which
becomes a set of values they do not approve of, which becomes your
entire being rendered as an "other".

It is for this reason that the average centrist party will become a
cheerleader of both far-right doctrine and conduct. At that point,
though, what could originally be justified as ignorance must now be
treated as complicity and, indeed, malevolence.

## The cultural vitality of youth

Europe is an ageing continent. The natives are literally dying because
they shifted to the urban life en masse, where the atomic family is
the upper limit of their daily community experience. They stopped
having extended families, which only really occur in rural areas with
the inclusion of grandparents, uncles/aunts, cousins, and neighbours
whose lineage is fully known.

This society-wide ageing is not merely a matter of people becoming
older. It has far-reaching cultural implications. Older people tend to
lose their cutting edge on all matters of collective living. They are
not as keen on experimentation because they are settled in their ways.
They become less open to new ideas and gradually stop searching for
fresh thinking altogether. They lose their physical fitness, which
prevents them from doing sport. They lack the playfulness for theatre,
fashion, art in general. They are jaded and disinterested. Thus
increasingly more facets of cultural experience are eroded until they
are washed away by the inexorable passage of time.

Youth provides a power impulse to culture at-large. Younger people
tend to be more interested in trying things out. To experiment with
new technologies, to entertain potentially useful ideas, to push the
boundaries wherever possible, to do sport, to play music, to organise
festivals. This practically means that there also are more job
opportunities for everyone.

For example, if I had thirty young lads in my mountains here in Cyprus
I could easily play football as a hobby (I am elite physically) and
even be their coach as a part-time job. Same idea for being their
language instructor or life mentor. But without the youth I have no
chance of getting a single interested party in these or practically
any other activity. And all I ask for is thirty individuals, not
thirty thousand. Even that tiny number is not available. Culture as a
quotidian affair is thus non-existent.

## The zombification of nations

Older people in communities that have plenty of youth get a second
wind in life. The generations interact with each other through shared
events. The elders provide experience and guidance while the younger
ones act as the spark of energy that makes things happen. A healthy
society has intergenerational solidarity, which contributes to its
vitality.

Conversely, the lack of youth leads to the zombification of the
nation. Europe's native populations are in this rapidly deepening
phase right now, which will become the driver for their increasingly
intolerant reflexes because of how old age makes society less
interesting overall.

The ideologues who dismiss any argument in favour of children as
"natalist", in a derogatory sense, those who are irresponsible and
selfish enough to interpret "family" as far-right dog whistles and who
thus give the extremists an easy win, do not realise how the
prevalence of old age kills culture.

Much like the ignorant middle class liberals vis-à-vis migrants, they
are not exposed to the insidious erosion of culture I mentioned above.
By the time they understand what is actually happening, they will be
zombies themselves.

## Europe's increasing irrelevance in global affairs

That Europe is punching below its economic weight in international
affairs is not a coincidence. Same for its Russophobia and Sinophobia.
Its decline is a function of the aforementioned. Without that spark of
vitality at the micro scale, there shall be no potent force at the
macro level.

Europe still has a lot of influence on the global stage, owning in
large part to its colonial legacy, but its relative weakening is
undeniable. As with many processes in life, change happens slowly
until it happens all of a sudden. This is the predicament Europeans
are in.

The continent is saddled in depth. Economic growth is frail. The
industrial base is weak. Competitiveness is missing. Social movements
have been infiltrated and inwardly corrupted by various brands of
individualism.

What the political elites have for a while now understood as their
short-term escape from this death spiral is the turn to the war
machine, as I explained in my previous essay about gigantism and
Europe's far-right transformation.

The elite's gambit is to antagonise Russia and China. Plus, with the
help of Israel, which remains their friend despite minor noises about
the occupied West Bank, they wish to exert wider control over the
Middle East and North Africa. This wider region, culturally defined by
its Muslim heritage, is showing strong signs of capacity for
independence and has the potential to break free from the clutches of
the Western empire.

## Europe's toxic cocktail of necessity and intolerance

Whatever they do, however, European societies require mass migration
to prevent their disorderly collapse. I state as much because I have
no faith that there can be a quick and thoroughgoing change in
attitudes that would inject new life from within.

The average European native is complacent in their ways and unwilling
to tolerate any kind of perceived discomfort, preferring instead the
road of short-term inertia that ends in longer-term annihilation.

Immigrants are the only force that can, in practice, invigorate
Europe: highly skilled experts but also working class people. Many of
those immigrants will be of a Muslim background, because Islam remains
a religion of vitality, in the sense of I have described herein. This
means that Europe is bound to change face in order to stay relevant or
submit to the rising powers of the world.

The problem, however, is that those immigrants are entering a society
of intolerant zombies. The native Europeans are not open to much
anymore and will only treat with hostility the growth of those migrant
communities.

They will pick totemic issues, such as the scarf Muslim girls wear to
school, in an attempt to enforce conformity with their own norms. They
will deny any wrongdoing, making this a matter of "liberation". Facts
are their first enemy. They will go after vagrants who purportedly are
a burden on the welfare state, only to end up targeting everyone. They
will not hesitate to become vigilantes who burn the Quran and who
generally use brutality to force people into submission. All to extend
their privilege for a little longer. And they will do it while still
claiming the moral high ground and touting their "European values".
