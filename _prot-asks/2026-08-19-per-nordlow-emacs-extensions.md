In this ~2-hour video I talk to Per Nordlöw about some of his custom
extensions for Emacs. I learn from Per how he approaches Emacs as a
tool that can be extended to meet the requirements of one's workflow.
In the process, Per shares his screen to show me extensions for
snippet expansions, linting and project diagnostics, and project
actions. There is so much more that Per has already implemented. It
can all be found here:

- <https://codeberg.org/nordlow/dotfiles>.
- <https://codeberg.org/nordlow/dotfiles/src/branch/main/.emacs.d>.
- <https://codeberg.org/nordlow/dotfiles/src/branch/main/.emacs.d/mine>.
